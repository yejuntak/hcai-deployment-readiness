# Feasibility plan for v0.1

Status: proposed; no recruitment, participants, approval, external review or results recorded here.

## Week 1: technical review
Author reviews the protocol, provisional gate, public attribution and licensing. Ask independent practitioners to desk-review instructions and the synthetic key when outreach is authorized. Record affiliation, relationship, specific sections, disagreements and changes. A favorable testimonial is not a substitute for a completed review.

## Week 2: prepare a bounded exercise
Obtain the institution's applicable determination before covered participant recruitment or data collection. Freeze tasks, consent/data arrangements, criterion, time budget, reference and analysis. The proposed 6-12 practitioner range addresses feasibility and is not a power calculation. Record reviewer qualifications and conflicts.

## Week 3: execute if permitted
Evaluate instruction comprehension, elapsed time, missing responses and reference-matching disagreements. Retain locked raw records and independently adjudicated matches. Report every recruited and completed instance and reasons for missingness. Use deidentified IDs; publish only permitted aggregate or deidentified material.

## Week 4: report and revise
Describe actual use, observed issues and revisions in v0.2. Link each change to a review or record ID. Separate internal application from independent use. If no pilot occurs, publish a development update with that status rather than a results report.

## Comparative claims require more
This kit supplies one instructional case, not matched cases or evidence of benefit. A comparison needs matched cases, prespecified assignment and analysis, measurement of prior exposure and carryover, and independent adjudication where possible. Do not infer causal impact from before/after remediation or a few favorable reviews.
